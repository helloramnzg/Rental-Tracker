# 10 – Business Rules

## Electricity Rate

The electricity rate is configurable by the landlord.

**Default Electricity Rate:** ₱15.00 / kWh

### Unit 1 Electricity

Electricity Usage = Current Reading − Previous Reading

Electricity Charge = Electricity Usage × Configured Electricity Rate

### Unit 2 Electricity

Electricity Charge = Mother Meter Bill − Unit 1 Electricity Cost

The configured electricity rate is applied consistently to all Unit 1 calculations.
