Bundled here (rather than loaded via next/font) so services/soa/generate-soa-pdf.ts
can embed real glyph outlines into generated PDFs at build/runtime,
including the Philippine Peso sign (₱, U+20B1), which pdf-lib's built-in
standard fonts do not support.
